Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2025

Introduction

Brillify ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website or use our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Service information — details about service requests, appointments, and scheduling preferences
  • Communication records — SMS messages, emails, and other communications related to service delivery
  • Usage data — information about how you interact with our website, including IP address, browser type, and pages visited

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and manage our services, including scheduling and appointment management
  • Send transactional communications such as appointment confirmations, reminders, and service updates via SMS and email
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Improve our services and website experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

SMS Messaging

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive SMS messages, we will use your number to send service-related text messages as described in our SMS Messaging Consent & Opt-In page. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.

We do not sell, share, or rent your phone number to third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers collected for SMS communications are used solely for the service-related purposes you consented to.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our business (such as messaging platforms and scheduling tools), but only to the extent necessary to provide our services. All service providers are bound by contractual obligations to protect your information.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Consent records for SMS messaging are retained for as long as needed to demonstrate compliance with applicable regulations.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@brillify.ai.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
